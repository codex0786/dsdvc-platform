# De Veirman, Cauberghe & Hudders (2017) — Instagram influencers, follower count and product divergence

**Claim.** In experiments, higher follower counts increased perceived popularity and likeability, but for divergent products a very high follower count could reduce brand attitude: influence is not simply proportional to followers. Aral & Walker (2012) show influence and susceptibility vary and can be estimated experimentally.

**In DSDVC.** `networks/influencers.py` separates audience size, engagement, relevance, trust, network position and conversion ability, and ranks by expected incremental conversions × LTV / cost with Monte Carlo intervals. The size penalty on trust/relevance is a documented prior.

**Not established.** Conversion or ROI effects of creator size.

## References
- De Veirman, M., Cauberghe, V., & Hudders, L. (2017). Marketing through Instagram influencers: the impact of number of followers and product divergence on brand attitude. International Journal of Advertising, 36(5), 798-828.
- Aral, S., & Walker, D. (2012). Identifying Influential and Susceptible Members of Social Networks. Science, 337(6092), 337-341.
