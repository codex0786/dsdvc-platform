# Watts & Dodds (2007) — Influentials, Networks, and Public Opinion Formation

**Claim.** In their simulated threshold models, large cascades were usually driven by a critical mass of easily influenced individuals rather than by a few highly influential 'influentials'.

**In DSDVC.** `agents/seeding.py` strategies (celebrity-first, micro-network, community seeding, hybrid) and `experiments/counterfactual.compare_seeding_strategies`, comparing reach, cost, cascade probability, conversion, retention and ROI with confidence intervals.

**Not established.** That celebrity seeding never works; the result is model-based, so DSDVC tests strategies case by case.

## References
- Watts, D. J., & Dodds, P. S. (2007). Influentials, Networks, and Public Opinion Formation. Journal of Consumer Research, 34(4), 441-458.
