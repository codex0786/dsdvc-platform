# Agent-based computational marketing

**Covered.** Guidelines for rigorous marketing ABMs — verification, validation, docking and sensitivity analysis (Rand & Rust 2011); ABC (Beaumont et al. 2002); simulation-based calibration (Talts et al. 2018); Sobol (Saltelli et al. 2010) and Morris (1991) sensitivity analysis.

**In DSDVC.** Legacy docking (v2/v3 preserved with regression tests), ABC/SBC calibration, Sobol/Morris/LHS designs, ablation and out-of-sample benchmarks, exact reproducibility records.

**Not established.** Validity of any particular DSDVC mechanism — that is what calibration and validation on observed data are for.
