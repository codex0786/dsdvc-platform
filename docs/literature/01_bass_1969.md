# Bass (1969) — A New Product Growth Model for Consumer Durables

**Claim.** First-purchase timing in a population of fixed size m follows the hazard h(t) = p + qF(t): an external (innovation) term p and an internal (imitation) term q proportional to cumulative adoption. The closed form fitted durables sales well with three parameters.

**Scope/assumptions.** Homogeneous, fully mixed population; constant market potential; no repeat purchase, price/advertising, competition, churn or network structure. The generalised Bass model (Bass, Krishnan & Jain 1994) multiplies the hazard by a marketing-mix term; Mahajan, Muller & Bass (1990) review further extensions.

**In DSDVC.** `diffusion/bass.py` (closed form, NLS, MLE, generalised Bass), `calibration/engine.py` (MCMC), the Bass *p* in `MarketSimulator`, and the macro benchmark every richer model must beat (`validation/benchmarks.py`).

**Not established.** Network, platform, STEPPS, churn or competition effects. DSDVC's ABM *extends* Bass; it does not claim the original assumptions hold.
